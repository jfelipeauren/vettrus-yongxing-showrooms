How to organize the display

Zone 1 • High turnover and easy shipping

  • Bands, ropes, supports, grip accessories, and recovery products.
  • Adjustable dumbbells, kettlebells, weight plates, and bars.
  • Mats, balls, ab wheels, and functional training kits.

Zone 2 • Demonstrable products

  • Exercise bikes, mini bikes, platforms, and compact equipment.
  • Benches, multi-gyms, racks, and modular accessories.
  • Display entry-level, mid-range, and premium versions.

Zone 3 • Private label

  • Standardize colors, logo, packaging, and weight families.
  • Apply Portuguese labels and manuals at origin.
  • Prepare kits for direct sales and fulfillment.

Zone 4 • Logistics engineering

  • Prioritize knock-down, stackable products with low cubic volume.
  • Require corrosion protection and reinforced packaging.
  • Test load capacity, welds, stability, and spare-parts availability.
