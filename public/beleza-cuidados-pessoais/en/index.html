Methodology

The category market size uses only each file's monthly Summary tab. Listing, Brand, Model and Seller rankings are independent views and are never added together. Product lines consolidate similar listings by category, brand and model across Sep/2025–Aug/2026. Sep/2026 is partial and is not included in 12-month totals. Priority weighs captured sales, units, recurrence, average ticket, visual appeal and import/showroom logistics.

Recommended use

Physically display priority A products. Use compact samples for bulky equipment and salon furniture. Keep priority C products as catalog extensions. Before purchasing, validate NCM, applicable ANVISA/Inmetro requirements, voltage, plug, battery transport, packaging, MOQ and landed cost in Brazil.
