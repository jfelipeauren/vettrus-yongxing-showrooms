Executive summary

Fishing is a complete ecosystem of core equipment, repeat-purchase consumables, organization, support, measurement and electronics. The best showroom should not merely repeat best sellers. It must show differences in engineering, finish, application and price range so Brazilian buyers can build complete product lines.

Recommended direction: allocate more space to reels, lures and hooks, lines, tackle boxes, snaps and holders. Display rods vertically with protection. Keep fish finders as a technology showcase. Treat spearguns as a technical line subject to regulatory and logistics validation.
