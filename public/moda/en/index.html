Executive summary

Fashion is a broad opportunity, but it requires disciplined curation. The best import strategy is not to “have everything”: it is to combine high-repeat travel accessories with controlled apparel and footwear capsules.

Recommended thesis

Showroom core: luggage, backpacks, travel bags, toiletry bags, wallets, sets and compact accessories. These are visually strong, standardizable lines with lower size-grid complexity.

Second layer: underwear, homewear, fitness, beachwear and basics, using sets and color/size families to raise average order value and reduce SKU dispersion.

Intelligence layer: footwear and trend-led fashion. Display proven references and materials, but negotiate pilot lots, short size runs and fast replenishment.

Important note

Decision framework

A

Buy and display

Compact, recurring and scalable lines. Build a complete wall of materials, colors, sizes and packaging solutions.

B

Test in capsules

Small lots, sets and coordinated collections; validate conversion, returns, size runs and creatives before scaling.

C

Use for intelligence

Keep samples and approved suppliers, but avoid deep inventory when brand, trend or size-grid risk dominates the decision.

Practical showroom plan

A four-wave rollout reduces dispersion and turns the showroom into a commercial tool, not merely a display.

1. Hero wallLuggage, backpacks, travel, bags and accessories. Show materials, hardware, capacity, sets and customization.
2. Capsule labFitness, underwear, pajamas, beachwear and basics. Display short families of styles, colors and size runs.
3. Footwear studioFootwear by construction and price tier. Work with prototypes, soles and pilot lots, without initial inventory depth.
4. China → CD MLDesign packaging, labeling, variants, barcodes and receiving standards at origin.

Methodology and traceability

Summary: market sizing by category. Listings: products and images. Brands: competitive recurrence. Sellers: concentration and operational references. Catalog and official-store views remain available in source files but were not added to other views.

Products were consolidated by normalized title within each category. Each card preserves the source file, seller and catalog indicator from the highest-sales record used as the visual reference.

Limitations: rankings are VirtualSeller Analytics estimates; the Top 100 listings, brands and sellers are independent rankings and must not be added together. Before importing, validate HS code, taxation, labeling requirements, intellectual property, quality, packaging, MOQ and landed cost.
