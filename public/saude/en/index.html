How totals were calculated

Monthly category size comes from the Summary tab. Products, volumes, tickets and references come only from the Listings tab.

Line sales and volume sum the Top 100 listings captured from September 2025 to August 2026. They are not total Mercado Livre GMV.

September 2026 is shown separately as a partial month.

Duplicate control

Listings, brands, sellers and catalog rankings are independent samples and were never added together.

The six subcategories are analyzed separately. Overlaps validate demand and are not added as one product.

Demand and growth derive from data. Private label, importability, regulation, logistics, showroom value and competition combine title evidence with commercial judgment.
