Physical curation plan

1. Technical wall and fast movers

Flashlights, headlamps, stakes, tensioners and accessories. Organize by function, output, charging, terrain and kit quantity. This is the highest SKU-density area per square meter.

2. Quick-setup zone

Automatic, beach, privacy and compact family tents. Demonstrate setup time, assembled dimensions and packed volume.

3. Folding furniture

Chairs, tables, cots, stools and sets. Display open and folded, highlighting load capacity, material, weight, fittings and carry bag.

4. Sleep and comfort

Sleeping bags, inflatable mattresses, sleeping pads and self-inflating mats. Create three tiers: entry, family and technical adventure, avoiding excess undifferentiated textiles.

5. Mobility and beach

Folding wagons with wide wheels, canopy, pet and cargo uses. Validate wheel performance, stability, stitching, actual capacity and shipping carton.

6. Purchasing criteria

Before ordering: NCM and taxation, packaging, MOQ, strength, corrosion, fabric, stitching, spare parts, Portuguese manual, labeling and landed cost in Brazil.

How totals were calculated

Monthly market size comes exclusively from each spreadsheet’s Summary tab. The consolidated period runs from September/2025 to August/2026. September/2026 is shown separately because it is partial.

Duplicate control

Repeated files for the same month were counted once. Listing, brand, seller and catalog rankings are independent samples and were never added together.

Product gallery

A listing’s sales and units represent the sum of captured Top 100 appearances over 12 months. Selection prioritizes format diversity, not sales alone.

Limits and validation

The data reflects VirtualSeller Analytics coverage, not Mercado Livre’s full GMV. Before importing, validate NCM, import duty, certifications, intellectual property, packaging, MOQ and landed cost.
