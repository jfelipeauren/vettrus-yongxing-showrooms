How totals were calculated

Monthly market size comes from the Overview sheet. September 2025 through August 2026 form the full 12-month window. September 2026 remains separate as a partial month.

Duplicate control

Listings were consolidated by normalized title within each macro. Listing, catalog, brand and seller rankings are independent samples and were never added together.

Subcategory variety

Listings were classified into semantic product families. The gallery uses round-robin selection: one product from each family first, then the second product from each family, and so on. This reduces visual repetition.

Recommended use

Before purchasing, validate vehicle application, HS/NCM classification, taxes, certification, intellectual property, safety, warranty, after-sales support, packaging, MOQ and landed cost in Brazil. Priority C items require enhanced technical due diligence.
